What Turn does that the big systems don't

⏱

Guests see the floor too

A live wait page built from the tables the camera sees, not a host's guess. Join from your phone and watch your place in line.

📷

No CCTV system needed

One laptop or phone camera runs it. No point-of-sale swap, no install crew, no monthly hardware.

🪪

Badges, not faces

Staff wear a printed badge, so the camera knows "staff" without knowing who. No face recognition, no per-person speed scores.

✋

Ask without waving

A table card flipped to HELP or CHECK is read by the camera and routed to that table's server.

How it works

  1. See. OpenCV 5 finds people at each table (NanoDet) and reads staff badges and table cards (ArUco markers).
  2. Decide. Each table moves through Seated, Needs bussing and Ready. Not greeted, Check in, Help and Check alerts are raised on timers and go red when overdue.
  3. Route. The alert goes to the server who owns that table, or to whoever has the lightest load. The next badge visit clears it automatically.
  4. Tell the guest. The wait page simulates seating the line at the soonest table that fits and shows an honest estimate.

Privacy by design

Faces are blurred in every frame Turn shows.

No frame is ever saved or sent anywhere. Turn keeps counts, not footage.

Staff are known by a badge number, never by face.

Load counts tables, not speed. Turn doesn't score people.
